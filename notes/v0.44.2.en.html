03Dungeons, rifts & bosses

The patch's core: the Buried Hoard (the treasure-map dungeons of World Quests) finally gets honest tuning.

One player or five, no surprises Tuning

Common = for one player. Rare, epic and legendary = for five, with NO party-size scaling. And rare+ bosses hit 30% softer, their adds 50%.

See details

Swap a partner mid-run New

Entry follows the opener's CURRENT party instead of the roster locked at the start: a disconnected player gets replaced without re-running the dungeon. Corpse recovery and already-earned rewards survive departures.

Vaults: time for a cleanup Fix

A vault attempt expires 6 h after digging, or 15 min after the last boss dies. No more stuck attempts cluttering your map.

See details

04World & quests

The world doesn't change; treasure maps finally say what they expect from you.

Talkative maps Interface

Every map shows its suggested player count, and entry messages now say when it's group content.

Quests unchanged Unchanged

The 8 minigames and three factions of v0.44.0 stay as they are.

05Guild & social

Discord is the patch's only social workstream — two fixes and one command.

Renamed roles fixed Fix

The Observer and Sentinel roles display correctly in game again after their Discord-side rename.

/flair: show or hide your tag New

/flair off hides your Discord role tag, /flair on shows it again, bare /flair reports the current setting. The preference survives logout.

06Quality of life & tech

One awaited repair and routine upkeep.

Character sheet repaired Fix

Stat tooltips on the character sheet are back on hover.

Upkeep Tech

Vulnerable dependencies updated and affected asset fingerprints refreshed — nothing visible in game.

What this means for you: if you dug maps solo, the commons stayed in your wheelhouse and the rest became genuinely playable at five without being overwhelming — the game even says how many it wants on each map. Your forgotten vault attempts clean themselves up, and your healer stops fighting their targeting bar.
