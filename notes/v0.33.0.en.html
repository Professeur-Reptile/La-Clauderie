The graphics & performance overhaul

The whole world has been re-textured, an "Insane" graphics tier now sits above Ultra, and 39 performance commits make it all run faster than before.

A re-textured world Overhaul

Surface-material families (bark, fabric, metal, rock) with real normals and roughness, combed grass with hue drift, cleaned-up dirt paths, cliff-base scree and textured canopies.

What changes on screen

The "Insane" tier Settings

Five presets — Low, Medium, High, Ultra, Insane — plus an Advanced mode: every dial adjusts separately, and dynamic resolution keeps big fights smooth.

The new settings

Prettier AND smoother Perf

39 optimization commits: fewer draw calls, SMAA edge antialiasing, front-to-back sorting, and zero per-frame memory allocations.

Every ability now has its own signature

No more one-size-fits-all colored particles: all 296 of the game's abilities each get a visual AND audio identity.

296 abilities, 296 visual identities VFX

An effects registry covers every one of the 296 abilities: animation archetypes, signature motifs, per-school impacts, ground decals — with dedicated identity passes for warrior, rogue, priest and druid.

How it's built

How hits feel SFX

Contact-frame hitstop, a windup lean during casts, a glow on the character rig — and a sampled audio layer with a per-ability sound identity.

03

The Wildheart Basin goes Heroic

The Palmreach caldera becomes the game's sixth heroic instance — with the most generous loot table of any five-man: two guaranteed epics per kill.

Heroic Zulgar Dungeon

Heroic Zulgar pays exactly two distinct epics per kill: one drawn from each group of three (33-34% per item) — the format every other heroic five-man uses.

The loot rules

Two blue mounts 0.1%

The only five-man offering both blue reins: Grag-Bear and Stalk-Glider, each at 0.1% on the heroic kill — the same rate as their original sources.

Why both, and at what rate

Worth knowing

  • As in the other heroic instances, epic and rare drops made inside the Basin can roll into upgraded heroic variants.
  • Epic mounts (80%) remain exclusive to rank-S Rift clears — nothing changes there.
04

Open world & Rifts: two exploits down

Mobs now know how to give up on an unreachable target, Rift bosses no longer stack their mechanics — and the normal-mode Basin gets its tuning pass.

Classic Evade Anti-exploit

An engaged mob that can't reach its target for 5 seconds walks home, immune and healed to full — the word "Evade" floats on screen. Killing a mob pinned behind a ledge is over.

The exact rules

Rift bosses: spaced mechanics Rifts

A Rift boss can no longer land two mechanics at the same instant (the same-tick fear + ground AOE combo): a shared lock enforces 5 seconds between any two mechanics.

How the lock works

The normal Basin, tuned Dungeon

The Fanglord Beastmaster drops his Beastspear at 12%, Zulgar guarantees an uncommon leg piece per kill, and an exit portal opens on his death.

The full tuning pass

Classes: the Mage rebalanced, the Warrior repaired

Two changes that matter: Greater Invisibility no longer protects you while invisible, and a warrior ability lost for months becomes learnable again.

Mage: Greater Invisibility reworked Balance

The 90% damage reduction no longer applies WHILE you're invisible for 20s: it applies for 2s after the invisibility ends. And Frost's "frozen target" bonus now counts every hard crowd-control, not roots alone.

Warrior: Seething Fury finally learnable Fix

The ability existed in the code but no warrior could learn it since the talent rework: it joins the kit at level 10 — 20 instant rage, 30s cooldown, off the GCD.

07

The game lands on the Epic Games Store

Epic becomes a first-class distribution channel — with an optional account link that mirrors your deeds into Epic achievements.

Linking your Epic account Platform

The principle is "link, not login": your game account stays in charge, and the optional link — a card in the settings — mirrors your deeds into Epic achievements.

How it works

Worth knowing

  • Nothing changes for existing accounts: linking is entirely optional.
  • Epic is never an authority: the game decides your deeds, Epic only receives a copy.
  • The desktop app can now be distributed and updated through Epic.
08

Interface, comfort & fixes

112 fixes ship alongside the overhaul — here are the ones that genuinely matter, plus the interface extras.

Interface Comfort

  • Configurable target aura windows: position, size, opacity and color of the icon, the arcs and the ground ring.
  • Each player's guild name now shows beside their name on both high-score boards — La Clauderie on top, naturally.
  • The market's armor filter gains neck and ring categories.
  • Complete translations across the game's 21 languages.

Notable fixes 112 fixes

  • Your offhand no longer loses its enchant, masterwork stats and upgrades when you log back in — they were being silently wiped on load.
  • The split-second black flashes in online play are fixed.
  • Drag-and-drop now correctly recognizes offhand slots.
