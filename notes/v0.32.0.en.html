01

Rifts: dungeons without end

The heart of the patch: fully procedural instanced dungeons, rebuilt identically from three numbers — a seed, a level, a floor index. Four portal ranks, eight environments, and a hand-authored citadel hiding in some seeds.

Born from a seed New

Every rift is generated from seed + level + floor, nothing else: server, offline sim and client rebuild exactly the same dungeon. Each descent runs 3 to 6 floors, with an environment rolled per floor.

How it works

Ranked C to S Challenge

Four portal ranks — C (lvl 20), B (22), A (25), S (28). The higher the rank, the more mechanics bosses reveal: 1 at C, up to 4 at S, with increasingly muscular heroic tuning.

The difficulty ladder

Loot follows the rank Loot

A guaranteed item from rank C, a guaranteed epic from B up — and at rank S, two exclusive legendaries at 0.3% each: Heart of the Rift and Voidsong.

Eleven realms to explore

The world grows all at once: eleven new realms, 105 quests, 48 new NPCs — and an open-field dungeon hidden behind a sunken idol.

The eleven newcomers New

From the Veiled Hollow to the Farshore by way of the Drakelands and the Evergarden: eleven realms, each with its own quests and inhabitants.

The full list

The Wildheart Basin Dungeon

A level-20 open-field dungeon hidden behind the Sunken Idol in the Palmreach: a flooded jungle caldera, two routes around a beast island, and a ritual terrace beneath a colossal jaguar shrine.

Guided tour

Worth noting

  • 105 new quests and 48 new NPCs spread across the eleven realms.

  • An interactive continent overview joins the world map to help you find your way.
03

Mounts become items

The persistent selection is gone: reins are ordinary usable items. Keep them in a bag, drag them to an action bar, click to mount.

Reins in your bag Rework

All six mounts now exist as reins items: bag, action bar, one click and you're mounted. Switching mounts is instant, dismounting needs no channel — and riding training remains the prerequisite.

The system in detail

Worth noting

  • Three speed tiers: uncommon +70%, rare +75%, epic +80%.
  • The epic reins (Aether-Jouster Hover-Cycle, Thunderstrut the Grand Gobbler) only drop from S-rank rifts.
  • The trusty Valorsteed is still the stablemaster's purchase — it has never been a drop.
04

A world streamed in

Chunk streaming replaces loading the world in one block: you see further, memory stops overflowing, and terrain fills in the blank spots on the map.

The world in chunks Perf

The world now loads chunk by chunk around you instead of residing whole in memory: greater view distance with no memory ceiling — it's what makes eleven realms at once possible.

What it changes

Worth noting

  • Procedural terrain fills the spaces between hand-authored zones — no more void between two regions.
  • In flight, the pass-over threshold is raised to grounded stride height: what you'd step over on foot no longer snags you at low altitude.

Meters your way Comfort

The combat meter panels are movable, resizable and separable — and hovering a row opens the damage breakdown, spell by spell.

Voices everywhere Audio
