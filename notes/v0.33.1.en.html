What's new in v0.33.1

The game's biggest visual update yet: a new Insane graphics tier, a full terrain and foliage pass, and above all 296 abilities that finally have their own visual and audio identity. On the gameplay side: mobs that can't reach you now Evade classic-style, the tank is no longer feared by their own boss, the Wildheart Basin answers as one — and open-world trash farming got a lot slower. 01

The world looks better — and runs better

One more tier at the top, a regated Medium, granular dials for tinkering yourself, and 39 optimization commits under the hood. v0.33.1 then goes and recovers the frame pacing the overhaul left on the table.

A new "Insane" tier New

Above Ultra: everything on. New surface-detail material families (bark, fabric, metal, rock), a full terrain and foliage pass, and a dynamic-resolution governor that lowers resolution instead of dropping frames.

The tiers in detail

v0.33.1 recovers the frame pacing Fix

Three sores left by the overhaul: stale on-screen regions on Medium, a shader prewarm that showed itself instead of staying behind the loading cover on desktop Insane, and GPU work colliding at boot. All three are fixed.

What was repaired

What changes to the eye

  • De-carpeted dirt paths, macro variation on every ground splat layer.
  • Combed grass with hue drift and a near-field blade carpet.
  • Cliff scree scatter (solid, and you can stand on it) and needle/leaf clumps in the canopies.

What changes in performance

  • Draw-call diet for props, shadows and world batches.
  • SMAA replaces supersampled edge AA.
  • Front-to-back opaque sort, Uint16 terrain indexing, frozen static foliage matrices.
  • No more per-frame allocations in the render loop.

Where to tune it

  • The granular dials let you raise one thing (grass, foliage) without raising everything.
  • The governor stays armed on every tier; Ultra and Insane simply give it more slack.
  • Insane pushes surface detail to 4 taps, against 3 on Ultra and 0 below.
02

Every ability now has a signature

No more interchangeable projectiles: a per-ability effect registry covers 296 abilities, each with its own bolt DNA, its impact by magic school, its ground decal — and its own audio identity.

296 abilities, 296 identities New

Spell effect events now carry the ability id, so every projectile keeps its authored look all the way to impact. Ground casts claim at their aimed point, zone pulses re-anchor to the zone.

How the system works

What you'll feel

  • Contact-frame hitstop: the hit has weight, the action freezes for a fraction of a second on impact.
  • A windup lean spring, character rig glow while casting and under buffs.
  • Per-class identity rounds: Shieldcrack, Reaping Arc, Jawcrack, Throat Wire, Bleed Out, Dirt Toss, Splitshot, Lingering Grace, Fleet Form.
  • A sampled SFX layer over each ability's procedural audio, plus staged female player voice recordings.
03

Mobs that cannot reach you now Evade

Classic behaviour is back: a mob pinned behind a wall or under a ledge while you chip it down risk-free walks home, heals to full — and you lose your loot rights.

Five seconds pinned, then home it goes Mobs

Detection is strict: the mob must be engaged, out of reach, committing zero movement, with its straight step toward you refused by geometry. Five seconds of that and it leaves — full heal, loot gone.

The exact conditions

Worth knowing

  • This applies to bosses too: pinning a boss out of reach force-resets the pull. Only Nythraxis opts out (encounter script).
  • Root holds the clock — no growth, no reset. Root spam can neither trigger an evade nor re-arm a pinned mob's timer.
  • A caster standing at spell range on purpose is never caught by this: that's a fight, not a stall.
  • This was the rift ledge exploit: it's closed.
04

The Wildheart Basin answers as one

Pulling Zulgar with the route still alive already woke the whole basin — except the far packs walked home before taking a single step. Not any more.

The punish finally reaches the whole basin Dungeon

A mob woken at the far end of Wildheart started already outside its own leash and evaded on its very first tick. It now carries a transit grace that suspends the soft leash while it crosses.

How it works

And a boss exit portal

  • A portal opens on Zulgar's death, at the shrine terrace — no more walking the ~220 yards of route back.
  • The accepted consequence of the grace: a group that pulls then runs gets chased, not leashed. A mob kited away from the pull point never spends its grace.
  • The two outs are unchanged: the exit portal (which scrubs you from every inside hate table) or a wipe. Both cost the group the attempt.
05

Rift bosses, fear, and mailboxes at last

Two combat fixes that genuinely change group life, and a handful of conveniences that were a long time coming.

Mechanics no longer stack up Rifts

On a rift boss, mechanics share a 5-second lock. One that comes due while the lock runs holds without re-arming — and when the lock clears, the most overdue one fires.

What's governed, what isn't

The tank is no longer feared Global

The boss's current target is exempt from its AoE fear: fearing the one player holding the boss sent it careening through the group. This covers every AoE-fear carrier, rift and dungeon alike.

The edge cases

Conveniences Quality of life

  • A mailbox in all eleven zone hubs: Eldergleam, Wyrmwatch, Icemantle, Lanternmere, Bridgemere, Moonrest, Gallowmere, Drifthaven, Hedgewick, Wickharbor, Gullhaven.
  • Items keep their identity through vendor buyback, the World Market and mail.
  • Guild names show beside each player on both high-score boards.
  • A target aura window, plus configurable combat auras (warrior proc auras included).

07

Trash farming got noticeably slower

The least spectacular change in the update and probably the one you'll feel first: open-world trash respawn moves from a flat 25 seconds to a per-zone tier.

60 / 120 / 180 seconds by zone Economy

A farmed camp regenerated about three times a minute and paid far past what its zone was tuned for. Now: 60s up to level 7, 120s through 14, 180s above.

What moves and what doesn't

And the coin curve

  • Coin rewards across the ten July zones were recalibrated: small trims on trash, sharp cuts on some elites (Galecrest's Gravekeep goes from 450 to 100 copper).
  • In the other direction: Wildheart mobs pay more (the Fanglord Beastmaster goes from 1,000 to 2,500 copper).
  • Named rares and minibosses keep their exact cadence: their clocks were not re-based.
08

Classes, fixes, and Epic Games

112 fix commits on the 0.33.0 line, concentrated in render (28) and sim (24). Three touch classes directly — including a warrior ability that was flat-out unfindable.

Warrior: Seething Fury exists at last Warrior

The ability was written, learnable at level 10… and attached to nothing since the talent rework. A level 20 warrior could not find it. It's now re-homed on the base kit.

The three class changes

Mage & warlock

  • Greater Invisibility: the 90% damage cut no longer applies during the vanish — it starts when the vanish ends, for 2 seconds. The 2-DoT strip is unchanged.
  • The warlock's tank demon auto-taunts by default: no manual toggle needed for it to hold aggro.
  • Fixes: frozen equip slots, mage frozen invisibility, plus a run of auth, warlock, mage, professions and social fixes.

Epic Games Distribution

  • Epic becomes a first-class distribution channel in the desktop builds.
  • Server-side account linking, with link-proof verification.
  • An achievement mirror to Epic, and a link card in the UI.
