What’s new in v0.44.0

The biggest release in months: World Quests with three allied factions, Warfare season 2 with 27 honor sets, the Thundercall shaman rework, a re-cut heroic loot table — and death that talks back.

Released 28 September 2026From v0.43.3Major release · Android 62, iOS 69

01Classes & spells

The shaman is the star, the druid finishes its metamorphosis, and raid tanks finally reach parity.

Thundercall, rebuilt from the ground up Shaman

A new resource, Thunder, and three brand-new spells — including Magma Burst, the shaman’s first fire spell.

See the detail

The druid grows paws Druid

Stalk, Lunge and Bruin Rush now work from any form, and Lunge jumps to 25 yd off the GCD.

See the detail

Raid tanks catch up Balance

Oathward (paladin) now doubles all threat generated, and the Ironguard warrior’s mastery climbs too: live raid parses had paladins at 234 threat/s against the warrior’s 330.

Elsewhere Other classes

Choirmend (ex-Prayer of Healing) heals allies within 30 yd for Benison priests; Redharvest, the Wildfang druid’s new finisher at 3 Old Blood, gets its own on-screen staging.

02Crafting & gathering

The biggest delivery of craftables in several releases.

5 new recipes Recipes

Elixir of Mana Regeneration and Potion of Invisibility (alchemy), a reinforced armor kit (leatherworking), a dense sharpening stone (weaponcrafting) and the Clockwork Shock Bomb (engineering, 120–160 Nature damage in a 5 yd area).

7 new enchants Enchants

Four weapon etchings (Dawnfire +18 spell power, Dawn’s Benediction, Piston Drive, Riftwalker’s Grace), Forged Might gloves, Shadowstride boots and an offhand Spirit etching.

Stations become visible Comfort

Placed mobile profession stations now have a visible world object, and a queued Town Focus change is remembered.

03Dungeons, rifts & bosses

No new dungeon: the Crucible of the Last Spring raid re-cuts its heroic loot.

Heroic loot is re-cut Raid

Crucible weapons and offhands arrive in Heroic, and robe sigils move to their own roll — no more losing them to a weapon.

See the detail

Smoother raid entries Fixes

Switching a raid’s difficulty is instant again (the 5-minute lock is gone), a wiped boss resets cleanly, and Ignivar pre-warms its arena to avoid the frame drops on entry.

Raid tracking on show Tracking

The character select screen lists each character’s raid lockouts, grouped by kind and collapsible; the Reliquary finally counts heroic clears.

04World & quests

The heart of the release: the open world now has a permanent game.

World Quests arrive New

Rotating activity boards with eight minigames, Clue Scrolls to solve, and treasure maps in four qualities.

See the detail

Three allied factions Reputation

The Rift Watch (Drifthaven), the Church Order (Brother Aldric) and the Automatons (Wyrmwatch) each have a standing ladder and quartermasters — the epic Viridian Valestrider mount sells at Rift Watch Champion, and the weekly emissary feeds the Weekly Vault.

Ferries bridge the coast Travel

Scheduled ferries connect Eastbrook and Wickharbor, each with a Harbormaster’s House; Farshore’s shipwrecks can be salvaged piece by piece.

Finished towns go quiet Quests

When every quest in a town is done, its zone-entry chat line changes — each of the three founding towns has its own.

05Guilde & social

Tools that set themselves up and repair themselves.

Custom guild ranks Guild

Create and order your own guild ranks with their permissions — imposed ranks are gone.

The Wanted board Market

The market gains a Wanted board: post buy orders, and a strip flags what isn’t trading right now.

The System Report Tool

The desktop client collects and saves a host diagnostic (frame rate ceiling included) to attach to a bug report.

PvP goes open-world PvP

The World PvP flag stakes gold and honor on open-field duels, King of the Hill arrives, and Warfare season 2 ships a 5-piece Vanguard honor set per spec (27 sets in total).

06Comfort & tech

Death becomes a conversation, and the interface is overhauled end to end.

Death becomes a dialogue Death

The Pale Keeper talks to you and asks for confirmation before resurrecting — and your ghost now knows where it died.

See the detail

The frame rate limit Tech

The game caps itself to hold your display’s cadence, on by default, with a manual ceiling option; a “camera ghost” graphics setting joins the options.

The interface gets tunable Interface

A Cooldown Manager, HUD presets, a movable mouseover tooltip, buff and debuff source lines, a death recap, the Action Cam, a usable trinket slot and the Weekly Vault: the big tidy-up is done.

Trading gets clickable Trading

Shift-click offers a chosen quantity, an Offer-all button handles the stack, the Materials Vault gains a name search, and /unstuck is free the first time each hour.

What this changes for you: there is now a game outside the dungeons — World Quests and their factions feed the daily loop, Warfare season 2 restarts the PvP, and Thundercall is a fresh start for the shaman. Numbers verified against the game’s code, as always.
