01The Crucible has its music

The raid had been running on the generic dungeon soundtrack. It now has its own, written for it, and split by floor.

Four tracks, five rooms Verified

The official notes say "four floors". The raid has five: the forge lift and the Halls of First Tempering share the same opening track. The other three each have their own.

See which room plays what

The music no longer switches to combat New

  • Everywhere else, entering combat moves the game to its combat theme.
  • Not in the Crucible: the floor's track keeps playing through pulls and through the boss.
  • Verified in the code, not just announced: while you are on a raid floor, the combat switch is disabled.
  • In practice a boss fight plays start to finish on one theme — which is the whole point of music written for an encounter.

And the rest Scope

  • Moving between floors fades into the next track, with no hard cut.
  • Leaving the raid restores the normal world soundtrack.
  • Volume, mute and the menu pause behave exactly as before.
  • Other dungeons and the Rifts keep their current soundtrack.

02And nothing else

This is the shortest release of the run: five files touched, all of them audio.

No game data changes Scope

  • No value for any spell, talent, mastery, set or item.
  • No mob, no quest, no zone.
  • The Builds guide, the PvP guide, the Professions, the Rifts and the Mounts pages stand as they are.

What still holds from v0.41.3 Reminder

  • Combat still sticks: you stay in combat while a living mob keeps you on its table.
  • In a dungeon, mobs never go home.
  • And the Hunter and Warlock win — the pet that no longer locks you — is still in place.
