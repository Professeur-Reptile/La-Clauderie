03Dungeons, rifts & bosses

Nythraxis, the game's king-boss, loses its signature phase 3 and learns patience; rifts close during trials.

Nythraxis: Bone Storm is retired Tuning

The boss's signature phase 3 never starts anymore. And Grave Eruption now waits out Soul Rend marks.

See details

Rifts closed during trials Fix

No rift portal opens inside the Evergarden mazes anymore, and you can't be pulled into a rift during the wisp maze, the shadow or the glider trial.

04World & quests

The world doesn't change; a stealth quest unlocks its last step.

Duskweave: the 2/4 fixed Fix

The stealth quest used to stall at 2/4: the two carriers with the big detection circles can be pickpocketed again while suspicion stays under 50%.

Quests unchanged Unchanged

The minigames, factions and treasure maps of v0.44.2 stay as they are.

05Guild & social

The release's core: hills pay bounties, open-world death can be bought back, and your online status belongs to you — while Warfare changes currency.

Streak bounties on hills PvP

Killing a player on a streak pays more (up to 33 Honor at 7 kills); a player on a death streak pays less (down to 3). The announcer calls streaks zone-wide.

See details

PvP Resurrect New

Killed in the open world by another player (or hit within the last 10 seconds)? The death screen offers to raise you at the graveyard, full health, no new Toll.

See details

Warfare: S1 for gold, S2 rated Gear

The season 1 set now costs gold (100 g for the 7 pieces). Season 2 gains crit/haste, spell power and eight new jewelry pieces.

See details

/presence: choose who sees you New

Everyone, friends, or nobody: your presence in friends lists and the guild roster (zone and map position included) is one command away.

See details

Streaks counted fairly Fix

The flag streak only counts time alive in the open world: instances and deaths no longer grow your counter. Stealthed players vanish from dead players outside their party, and a ghost shows only to its own party.

06Quality of life & tech

Clearer combat cues, and the usual translation pass.

Your dots on the target frame Interface

Target dots now track YOUR damage-over-time effects and YOUR debuffs on hostile players — in battlegrounds, arenas, duels and flagged World PvP.

Translations Tech

All the new text (bounties, Resurrect, /presence) is translated into the game's supported languages.

What this means for you: hills become the stage for real stories — the one on a streak has a price on their head, the announcer chimes in, and dying no longer sends you across the world. Your online status is yours, the old Warfare set becomes a gold annuity, and Nythraxis fights without its storm: the site's DPS figures will be re-run accordingly.
