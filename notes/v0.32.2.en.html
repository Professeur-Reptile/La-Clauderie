What's new in v0.32.2

A comfort-and-fairness patch: rift portals now respawn without ever overlapping a run still in progress, an in-rift floor tracker and countdown show while you descend, party members appear on the map, perfectly-timed duels no longer end in a double death — and the game's textures are compressed for memory-tight mobiles. 01

Portals that finally behave

v0.32.1's hourly rotation could overlap a run still in progress. It's refined: every zone respawns at the first hourly boundary right after its previous portal closes — never before.

Respawn waits for closure Fairness

A portal now opens at the first hourly boundary after the previous one closes (sealed by a clear or collapsed after 2h) — never while a descent is still running.

The full mechanic

Where you are, how long you've got UI

Once inside, a tracker shows the current floor ("Floor 2 of 5") and a countdown to the entrance's closure — no more guessing how much time is left.

What's shown

Worth noting

  • An unclaimed portal's lifetime is now 2 hours (was 1h) — the hourly respawn cadence itself is unchanged.
  • A zone sealed by a first-clear stays empty until the first hourly boundary after the seal.
02

The map fills in, duels get decisive

Party members finally show up on the map, a simultaneous fatal blow in a duel no longer ends in a double death, and six rare mobs join the chronicle deeds.

Your party, on the map Comfort

Party members now show up on the zone map and the continent overview — no more asking "where are you" in chat.

What changes

One winner, never two deaths Fix

Two lethal blows landing on the same tick no longer cancel out into a double death: the resolution now picks a single winner, left at 1 HP.

The rule in detail

Worth noting

  • Six named rare mobs — including Grubjaw the Glutton and Old Cragmaw — now count toward four new chronicle deeds.
  • Escort quest ambush waves now re-engage on the escortee instead of leashing back home.
03

Mobile and backstage

Every texture in the game switches to a GPU-compressed format, built for memory-tight mobiles.

Compressed textures Perf

Every 3D model texture switches to KTX2/Basis, GPU-compressed: no more decode amplification forcing the game closed on iOS.

What it changes

Worth noting

  • Saved action bar layouts now correctly restore once in offline-world play.
  • Under the hood: 23,168 tests green, 1,404 files changed.
