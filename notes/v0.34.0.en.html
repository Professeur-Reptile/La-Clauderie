What's new in v0.34.0

The economy update. The guild bank arrives, mounts can finally be traded between players, the Drakelands are rebuilt, and professions get their biggest patch yet: tool charms, real fish cooking, twice the gathering nodes. And in Rifts, big boss attacks can now be seen coming. Click a card for details.

01

The guild bank is here

A shared chest for the guild: pooled gold and items, purchasable expansions, and a log that records everything. One big caveat to know before you get excited.

The shared chest New

A shared gold treasury (up to 100,000 gold) and an item store that expands to 60 slots, paid with guild funds. Every deposit, withdrawal and purchase is logged — everyone sees who did what. Well… everyone? See the caveat.

See details

⚠️ Officers only Important

  • Only the guild master and officers can deposit, withdraw — or even SEE the bank. Members get no tab, no read access, no log. We checked the code: it's by design.
  • The activity log (last 50 operations) is also officer-only.
  • In practice for the guild: hand deposits to an officer, and ask them for the chest's state.
02

Mounts can be traded

The most visible economy change: reins are no longer soulbound. A mount can be sold, gifted, mailed — like any other item. Details and traps in the updated Mount guide.

Reins are for sale Economy

Direct trade, mail, World Market, guild bank: every reins in the game circulates, including the ones already in your bags. Whoever holds the reins owns the mount.

See details

⚠️ The Raptor: not farmable Warning

The update's flagship mount, the Drakemaw Raptor, shows up in game but can't be obtained anywhere — its drop was pulled right before release. We checked the code. The Terrorspark still can't either.

Why

Comfort Handy

  • On mobile, the More tray gains a mount button (summons your first owned mount).
  • Careful: listing the reins you are currently riding dismounts you on the spot.
  • Vendor-selling and discarding stay blocked — no mount can ever be destroyed by mistake.
03

The Drakelands, rebuilt

The old drakes are gone: The Drakelands is repopulated by the Dragonkin brood, with two new chronicle deeds to earn.

The Dragonkin brood Mobs

Eggs that hatch into whelps if you ignore them, patrolling broodguards, and four elite Drakemaw Broodlords — plus Cindraleth, the crater matriarch, fully reworked.

See details

Two deeds Chronicle

Clutch Breaker (kill a Broodlord) and The Sky Goes Quiet (finish the Matriarch's quest chain) — 10 Renown each. The second is retroactive if you've already done the quest.

The exact conditions

And elsewhere Elites

  • Grubjaw (Mirefen): a new giant model — same stats, same loot.
  • Two new world elites: The Drowned Warlord (Mirefen, lvl 12) and Brakka the Wallbreaker (Thornpeak, lvl 17).
  • Unannounced bonus: Threnos the First Voice (Thornpeak, lvl 19), who drops a rare cloak at 6%.
04

Professions: their biggest patch ever

Tool charms crafted by enchanters, fishing that goes through the kitchen, and gathering twice as dense. The Gathering & Professions page is up to date.

Tool charms Enchanter

Two new enchanter-crafted items: Gatherer's Cache (doubled harvests) and Artisan's Eye (fine grade). The twist: a charge is only spent when the bonus actually mattered.

See details

Fishing goes to the kitchen Fishing

Catches are now raw: you can't eat them, you cook them (6 new recipes). And every body of water has a teaching ceiling — past it, your rod teaches you nothing.

See details

Gathering: denser, slower Gathering

Node count jumps from 99 to 156 (six zones tripled)… but each node regrows in 4 minutes instead of 2. And tools now require a profession level to be used.

See details

Rifts: bosses you can finally read

No more unavoidable damage: everything telegraphs, everything dodges. The Rift guide is up to date.

Big attacks can be seen coming Rifts

A ground ring 1.2s before every detonation, sweeps that only hit in front of the boss, and on-hit control suspended while you escape. Rift bosses only.

See details

Delves: the chest can't jam anymore Delves

Failing every lockpicking try on the final chest no longer locks it forever: it opens anyway, with base-tier loot. Picking it cleanly still pays much better.

06

The world: farming, quests, towns

v0.33 slowed farming down; v0.34 reshuffles the deck again — in both directions. And quests cheat less.

Respawns change (again) Economy

World mobs now return in 60 seconds everywhere — we told you 1 to 3 minutes in v0.33, and that's been collapsed. But Drogmar got nerfed, and gathering nodes go the other way.

See details

Quests cheat less Quests

No more ringing the same bell three times: each interactable object only credits once. Quest items are marked in your bags, and repeatable work orders show in blue.

See details

The towns Scenery

  • Fenbridge (Mirefen) is fully rebuilt: connected boardwalks, cleared exits, a real marsh town.
  • Gull Mere, the official notes' "fishing town", is actually… a lake: the first fishable water on The Farshore island, next to the hamlet of Gullhaven.
  • When you die, the game finally tells you who (or what) killed you.
07

For your class

A big mage nerf, three rogue repairs, and an important clarification about the druid's bear.

Mage Nerf

  • Collective Reversal (the after-wipe group res, 30% HP) gains a 5-minute cooldown — it had none.
  • Why: a backline mage could drop out of combat mid-fight and chain mass resurrections. It's a recovery tool again, not a strategy.

Rogue: three repairs Fix

Smokestep finally works (attacks already engaged actually stop), Borrowed Tempo no longer eats your combo points, and a stunned player can no longer turn — your back position is guaranteed.

See details

Druid & misc Good to know

  • Bruin Form has a real four-legged bear model. It's purely visual: druid tanking already existed, armor and threat are unchanged.
  • Resurrection sickness (−75% stats) can no longer be dispelled — by anything. Only time removes it.
  • Double-clicking food no longer wastes a second copy, and dead players can no longer perform profession actions.
08

Comfort, interface and the rest

Lots of small things that add up to a big daily difference.

Interface Handy

  • Chat tabs reorder by dragging, and deeds are clickable in chat.
  • Mouse buttons bind like keys, an on-bar key-binding mode, and action bar slot locking.
  • The bank gains a "deposit all materials" button that lists what it will transfer.

Smarter shopping Vendors

  • At vendors: buy by 1, 5, 10 or any custom amount (mounts and soulbound items stay one at a time).
  • The Market asks for a buy confirmation — and re-checks the price when you validate.
  • The Dungeon Finder hides listings for dungeons you're locked out of.

And the rest Global

  • Over 230 hand-painted images replace generated icons (items, spells, buffs, portraits) — the Codex and this site already show them.
  • An official Discord bot syncs members and roles, and mirrors guild activity.
  • The game is translated into 21 languages, admin dashboard included.
