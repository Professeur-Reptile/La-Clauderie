01Quality of life & tech

The entire update fits under this one heading — rare enough to say out loud. Three performance jobs, two of which you can see on screen without losing any readability.

Mail stops weighing on the realm Server

Standing at a raven pillar re-sent your entire mailbox, letter bodies included, twenty times a second. It is now four times a second, and only when something actually moved.

See the details

Weapon effects fade with distance Rendering

The visual effects on weapon skins lose brightness with distance, down to 40% of their authored strength. A fade, never a disappearance — and nothing that matters in a fight is touched.

See the details

Ground marks lighten the bill Rendering

Spell auras and decals hug the terrain point by point. At range the game measures less than a third of them — without the mark’s footprint moving an inch.

See the details

Under the hood Performance

  • The commission order board was read in full, for every session, every tick: it too drops to four times a second and on change only.
  • Effect anchors now resolve without allocating every frame, and geometry uploads send only what the frame actually wrote instead of the buffer’s whole capacity.
  • A weapon effect already hidden by the far-distance silhouette swap no longer burns frame time on something no pixel can show.

Behind the scenes Maintenance

  • The tick profiler finally breaks the player-data broadcast down by key group: the next drift of this kind will be named by a capture rather than deduced. It costs nothing outside a capture.
  • A vulnerable dependency (nanoid) is pinned to a fixed version.
  • The admin console is complete across its 21 locales — that is the administrators’ tool, not the game client.

02So what about the game?
